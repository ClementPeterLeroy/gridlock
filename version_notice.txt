GRIDLOCK - DEVELOPMENT VERSION HISTORY
Current version: 2.1

VERSION 1.0 - ORIGINAL GAME

Setting
Gridlock is a local, turn-based strategy board game for two players sharing
one computer. Built with Python and Pygame, it takes place on a 9 x 9 board.
Players race to the opposite side while placing walls to slow each other down.

Rules
- Blue starts in the middle of the bottom edge; Red starts in the middle
  of the top edge. Blue takes the first turn.
- Each player starts with 10 walls.
- On each turn, either move your pawn or place one wall.
- Move one square horizontally or vertically, provided no wall blocks it.
- When an opponent is directly adjacent, jump over that pawn if the square
  behind it is free and reachable without crossing a wall.
- If the jump is blocked by a wall or the board edge, step diagonally around
  the adjacent pawn instead, using an unobstructed route.
- Pawns cannot share a square or move through walls.
- A wall spans two square edges and can be horizontal or vertical.
- Walls cannot overlap or cross. Every player must retain a possible path
  to their goal edge; completely trapping a player is forbidden.
- Placing a wall spends one wall from your supply. Placed walls stay put.
- The first player to reach any square on the opposite edge wins.

VERSION 1.1 - BUG FIXES AND TURN VISIBILITY

- Fixed bugs in the original game.
- Added a highlight to the cell underneath the current player's pawn,

VERSION 2.0 - GAME MODES AND PRESENTATION

Game modes and menu
- Added a main menu for choosing a game mode.
- Versus: the standard two-player game, with 10 walls per player and an
  elapsed turn timer without time penalties.
- Battle Royale: four local players, with 5 walls each. Blue starts at the
  bottom, Green at the left, Red at the top, and Violet at the right.
  Turns run clockwise: Blue, Green, Red, Violet. Each player must reach
  the opposite edge; the first to do so wins. Jumps cannot land on another
  pawn. A blocked or occupied landing square allows a legal sidestep.
- Blitz: two players, with 180 seconds each. Only the active player's clock
  runs. At the start of a turn, remaining time below 10 seconds is raised
  to 10 seconds. Running out of time costs one wall and grants 15 seconds
  to finish the turn. Running out again during that turn loses the game.
  Running out with no walls available also loses the game.
- Rise of the Machines: menu entry reserved for a future AI mode;
  currently starts a regular two-player Versus game.
- Settings: placeholder menu entry; configuration is not implemented yet.

Visuals and controls
- Added victory screens with color-specific stickers for Blue, Red, Green,
  and Violet. SVG originals and PNG versions are bundled with the game.
- Added highlighted legal destinations when selecting the active pawn.
- Added wall placement previews, rotation with R, and cancellation with Esc.
- Added Undo / Ctrl+Z to restore the previous completed move, including
  stored clocks, wall supplies, and Blitz penalty state.
- Added New game and Menu controls. On victory, N starts a new match
  and M returns to the menu.
- The active player's cell remains highlighted in gold.

Maintenance
- Added automated checks for movement, jumps, walls, goal detection,
  four-player turn order, undo behavior, and Blitz timing.

VERSION 2.1 - QUALITY OF LIFE & AUDIO UPDATE

- Added right-click to rotate the wall preview by 90 degrees during wall
  placement. The R shortcut remains available.
- Kept player-specific victory stickers and removed redundant winner text.
- Added clickable Play Again and Main Menu buttons to the victory screen.
  Play Again restarts the same mode; N and M shortcuts remain available.
- Centered the explanation of timeout defeats beneath the victory sticker.
- Added continuous, low-volume background music through pygame.mixer.
  Menu and match transitions do not restart the track.
- Added a sound for successful pawn moves and a one-time match-end sound.
  The victory audio file's extension is detected automatically.
- Enabled Settings with immediate Music and Sound Effects toggles, plus an
  Animations toggle reserved for future features. Settings persist for the
  current application session.
- Added Exit Game to the bottom-right of the main menu.
- Preserved the wooden visual style, existing modes, rules, clocks,
  wall penalties, and turn order. Rise of the Machines remains a
  two-player placeholder without AI.
- Added interface tests for rotation, settings, move audio, timeout audio,
  victory navigation, and clean exit.
- Audio assets are development placeholders. The music is not final,
  optimized, or tailor-made for Gridlock yet.
