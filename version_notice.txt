GRIDLOCK - DEVELOPMENT VERSION HISTORY
Current version: 2.7

VERSION 1.0 - ORIGINAL GAME

Setting
Gridlock is a local, turn-based strategy board game for two players sharing
one computer. Built with Python and Pygame, it takes place on a 9 x 9 board.
Players race to the opposite side while placing walls to slow each other down.

Rules
- Blue starts in the middle of the bottom edge; Red starts in the middle
  of the top edge. Blue takes the first turn.
- Each player starts with 10 walls.
- On each turn, either move your pawn or place one wall.
- Move one square horizontally or vertically, provided no wall blocks it.
- When an opponent is directly adjacent, jump over that pawn if the square
  behind it is free and reachable without crossing a wall.
- If the jump is blocked by a wall or the board edge, step diagonally around
  the adjacent pawn instead, using an unobstructed route.
- Pawns cannot share a square or move through walls.
- A wall spans two square edges and can be horizontal or vertical.
- Walls cannot overlap or cross. Every player must retain a possible path
  to their goal edge; completely trapping a player is forbidden.
- Placing a wall spends one wall from your supply. Placed walls stay put.
- The first player to reach any square on the opposite edge wins.

VERSION 2.0 - GAME MODES AND PRESENTATION

Game modes and menu
- Added a main menu for choosing a game mode.
- Versus: the standard two-player game, with 10 walls per player and an
  elapsed turn timer without time penalties.
- Battle Royale: four local players, with 5 walls each. Blue starts at the
  bottom, Green at the left, Red at the top, and Violet at the right.
  Turns run clockwise: Blue, Green, Red, Violet. Each player must reach
  the opposite edge; the first to do so wins. Jumps cannot land on another
  pawn. A blocked or occupied landing square allows a legal sidestep.
- Blitz: two players, with 180 seconds each. Only the active player's clock
  runs. At the start of a turn, remaining time below 10 seconds is raised
  to 10 seconds. Running out of time costs one wall and grants 15 seconds
  to finish the turn. Running out again during that turn loses the game.
  Running out with no walls available also loses the game.
- Rise of the Machines: menu entry reserved for a future AI mode;
  currently starts a regular two-player Versus game.
- Settings: placeholder menu entry; configuration is not implemented yet.

Visuals and controls
- Added victory screens with color-specific stickers for Blue, Red, Green,
  and Violet. SVG originals and PNG versions are bundled with the game.
- Added highlighted legal destinations when selecting the active pawn.
- Added wall placement previews, rotation with R, and cancellation with Esc.
- Added Undo / Ctrl+Z to restore the previous completed move, including
  stored clocks, wall supplies, and Blitz penalty state.
- Added New game and Menu controls. On victory, N starts a new match
  and M returns to the menu.
- The active player's cell remains highlighted in gold.

Maintenance
- Added automated checks for movement, jumps, walls, goal detection,
  four-player turn order, undo behavior, and Blitz timing.

VERSION 2.1 - QUALITY OF LIFE & AUDIO UPDATE

VERSION 2.3 - RISE OF THE MACHINES

- Enabled Rise of the Machines with a difficulty selection screen:
  Casual, Challenging, and Back to Main Menu.
- Added Pathfinder, a lightweight rule-based computer opponent controlling
  Red. The human controls Blue and moves first; both receive 10 walls.
- Casual favors movement, varies reasonable choices, and occasionally
  considers nearby walls. Challenging evaluates both players' BFS routes
  and all legal wall positions, favoring useful delays and wall conservation.
- Both difficulties take immediate wins and try to prevent immediate losses.
- Isolated search in ai/pathfinder.py. Proposed moves and walls are validated
  and executed through the existing game engine and shared rules.
- Search runs incrementally between frames, with a legal fallback, about
  4 ms of work per frame, and a maximum 10-second decision budget.
- Displayed Red - Pathfinder and the selected difficulty during play.
- Ignored human board input during computer turns while retaining menu,
  restart, undo, and window-close controls. Undo restores the human's turn.
- Play Again and New game preserve the selected AI difficulty.
- Preserved Versus, Battle Royale, Blitz, victory stickers, audio,
  settings, wall rotation, clocks, penalties, and turn order.
- Added AI legality, tactical, deadline, adjacent-pawn, zero-wall,
  full-match, and UI regression tests, plus a seeded comparison script.
- Known limitations: no deep lookahead or machine learning; BFS route
  estimates ignore temporary pawn obstacles, but legal move generation
  includes them. Difficulty strength is heuristic. Audio remains placeholder.
- Neural networks using a future backend are deferred to a later release;
  no PyTorch, TensorFlow, or Keras dependencies have been introduced.

VERSION 2.4 - SMART MOUSE CONTROLS

- Widened groove detection by 3 pixels on each side for easier aiming.
- Lightened golden wall previews and used Oxblood (#5C1F25) for placed walls.
- Moved the main-menu version label to the bottom-left.

- Replaced manual Wall Mode with automatic wall-hover previews and direct
  left-click placement. Removed the Wall button and expanded Undo to fill
  the freed space while preserving New game and Menu.
- Valid walls preview in translucent golden brown; invalid walls preview
  in red. Hovering off a groove immediately hides the preview.
- Restricted wall detection to actual board grooves to avoid previews or
  accidental placements over squares, pawns, and interface controls.
- Preserved right-click and R rotation. The chosen orientation persists
  across turns until changed. Preview validity updates after rotation.
- Clicking the active pawn selects it and shows green legal destinations.
  Clicking it again deselects it and restores automatic wall previews.
  Esc also clears selection. Illegal destinations and groove clicks keep
  movement selection active without placing walls.
- Successful moves and wall placements clear selection and end the turn.
- Disabled interactive wall previews with no walls left, during computer
  turns, and after victory. Only the active pawn is selectable in all modes.
- Reused the existing rules engine for placement validation and movement;
  Pathfinder's action interface and both difficulty algorithms are unchanged.
- Preserved rules, clocks, penalties, turn order, music, sounds, settings,
  victory screens, undo, restart, and all four game modes.
- Added direct-placement and selection regression tests across modes,
  plus headless Pygame rendering checks for preview colors and visibility.
- Known limitations: narrow grooves require accurate cursor placement.
  Away from groove intersections, previews require a groove matching the
  chosen orientation. Existing placeholder audio and AI limitations remain.

VERSION 2.5 - DAWN OF THE MACHINES

- Added a Battle Royale setup screen with Human, AI - Casual, and
  AI - Challenging controls for every seat, plus Start Match and Back.
- Default seats: Blue Human, Green Casual AI, Red Casual AI, Violet Casual AI.
  All-human, mixed, and all-AI configurations are supported.
- Extended the shared Pathfinder to all four goal directions, all opponents'
  BFS distances, and immediate threats from any opponent. Closest opponents
  receive priority; wall validation still preserves a route for every pawn.
- Added a legal wall fallback for temporarily blocked pawn corridors when
  no pawn move is available and the current player still has walls.
- Kept clockwise Blue, Green, Red, Violet turns and five walls per seat.
- Added a nonblocking three-second minimum wait before each AI action in
  both AI modes. Search overlaps the wait and retains its ten-second limit,
  incremental processing, and legal fallback proposal.
- Added per-player Human, Casual AI, and Challenging AI labels. Human board
  input and previews are disabled during AI turns; menu controls stay usable.
- Reused the normal game engine for AI execution, move/victory sounds,
  victory detection, and player-specific stickers.
- Play Again and New game preserve every seat and difficulty. Undo in mixed
  matches restores the previous human turn; all-AI undo reverses one action.
- Preserved v2.4 smart controls, Oxblood walls, lighter previews, bottom-left
  version label, wooden visuals, audio, settings, and the other game modes.
- Added controller tests for all 81 configurations, four-direction AI
  legality and tactics, deadlines, adjacent pawns, no walls, full games,
  nonblocking pacing, automatic turn advancement, and replay configuration.
- Automated four-AI check: 12 seeded games finished within 400 turns with
  no illegal actions or crashes; 815 decisions and 147 wall placements.
  Mean search 22.9 ms, maximum 112.8 ms, longest incremental step 1.8 ms.
  Measurements exclude UI pacing and are specific to this local test run.
- Known limitations: heuristic play has no deep multiplayer lookahead;
  simultaneous winning threats may not all be preventable in one action.
  BFS estimates ignore temporary pawn obstacles. Sampled matches do not
  establish balanced seat strength. Placeholder audio remains unchanged.

VERSION 2.6 - MATCH CUSTOMIZATION

- Unified setup for Versus, Battle Royale, Blitz, and Rise of the Machines,
  with relevant seat, name, difficulty, starting-player, and timer options.
- Added editable human names, limited to 12 characters with color defaults.
  AI seats use Pathfinder plus their difficulty. Configuration is session-only.
- Added optional uniformly random starting players. Restart rerolls the
  starter while preserving the preference and all other match options.
- Added a short cycling starter reveal with a final announcement; click,
  Space, Enter, or Esc skips it. Animations Off skips it automatically.
  Clocks and AI actions stay suspended until the reveal finishes.
- Added Untimed or 15/30/60/120-second turn limits to Versus and Battle Royale.
  Versus timeouts forfeit the match. Battle Royale removes the timed-out
  pawn and skips that seat while keeping placed walls; the last survivor wins.
- Kept stable player indices for goal directions and victory stickers after
  elimination; rules and Pathfinder ignore eliminated pawns and their paths.
- Added 1/3/5-minute Blitz starting clocks; preserved the ten-second minimum,
  one-wall penalty, 15-second compensation, and existing forfeit conditions.
- Added Pause via Esc and a visible button, with Resume Game, Restart Match,
  Settings, and Main Menu. Pausing freezes timers, AI search deadlines,
  presentation delay, and board input while keeping the window responsive.
- Preserved three-second AI pacing without charging presentation time to
  the AI's clock. Thinking is capped by the available turn time and ten
  seconds; paused time is excluded from its budget.
- Preserved names, seats, difficulties, timing options, and starting-player
  preference across New game, Restart Match, and Play Again.
- Improved incoming-turn highlights and fitted custom names, colors,
  controller labels, wall counts, and clocks into both player layouts.
- Added match_config.py to keep configuration separate from rules. Updated
  setup and event-loop tests and added name, randomized-order, clock,
  pause, timeout elimination, and surviving-player AI regression tests.
- Preserved Smart Mouse Controls, wooden visuals, Oxblood walls, settings,
  audio, victory stickers, all game modes, and both Pathfinder difficulties.
- Known limitations: session-only names/options, 12-character names with
  system-font coverage, simple animations, heuristic AI, and placeholder
  audio. No records, branding overhaul, or machine learning added.
